About ITI Katihar/

Welcome to Govt. ITI Katihar (Government Industrial Training Institute, Katihar, Bihar).

Govt. ITI Katihar is an established government technical training institution under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India, offering regular technical and vocational programs (such as Electrician, Fitter, Wireman, Turner, Machinist, etc.) aligned with the NCVT and National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF)

Lifetime Access

Certification: Upon successful completion of the course and clearing the All India Trade Test (AITT) conducted by NCVT, students receive a National Trade Certificate (NTC), which is recognized across India and internationally

Awesome Features

Government Industrial Training Institute (Govt ITI) in Katihar, Bihar, offers several standout features and technical programs

Diverse Technical Trades

The institute runs a wide range of NCVT-recognized technical and vocational trades, including Electrician (FT/ELEC), Fitter, Wireman (WM), Welder (WDR), Turner (TR), Draughtsman, Plumber (PLB), Motor Mechanic Vehicle (MMV), Information and Communication Technology System Maintenance (ICTSM), and more.

Industry-Ready Training

Workshops are equipped with practical tools and machinery to give students hands-on experience for immediate entry into the workforce.

Active Placement Drives

The institute regularly organizes campus placement programs and recruitment drives to connect trained youth with employment and apprenticeship opportunities.

Regulated by Government Authorities

Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)

Directorate General of Training (DGT), Government of India.NCVT Affiliation: It is officially recognized and affiliated with the National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT), meaning its certifications are standardized nationwide. State Recognition: Functioning as a premier state-run institute under the Department of Labour Resources, Government of Bihar (admissions channeled via BCECEB/ITICAT), ensuring complete legal and educational authenticity.
