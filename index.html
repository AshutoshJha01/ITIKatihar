Awesome Features

Government Industrial Training Institute (Govt ITI) in Katihar, Bihar, offers several standout features and technical programs

Diverse Technical Trades

The institute runs a wide range of NCVT-recognized technical and vocational trades, including Electrician (FT/ELEC), Fitter, Wireman (WM), Welder (WDR), Turner (TR), Draughtsman, Plumber (PLB), Motor Mechanic Vehicle (MMV), Information and Communication Technology System Maintenance (ICTSM), and more.

Industry-Ready Training

Workshops are equipped with practical tools and machinery to give students hands-on experience for immediate entry into the workforce.

Active Placement Drives

The institute regularly organizes campus placement programs and recruitment drives to connect trained youth with employment and apprenticeship opportunities.
