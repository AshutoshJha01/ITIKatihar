Why Choose Govt. ITI Katihar

Awesome Features

Government Industrial Training Institute (Govt ITI) in Katihar, Bihar, offers several standout features and technical programs designed for modern industrial careers.

Diverse Technical Trades

The institute runs a wide range of NCVT-recognized technical and vocational trades, including Electrician (FT/ELEC), Fitter, Wireman (WM), Welder (WDR), Turner (TR), Draughtsman, Plumber (PLB), Motor Mechanic Vehicle (MMV), Information and Communication Technology System Maintenance (ICTSM), and more.

Industry-Ready Training

Workshops are equipped with practical tools, heavy machinery, and modern diagnostic instruments to give students hands-on experience for immediate entry into the industrial workforce.

Active Placement Drives

The institute regularly organizes campus placement programs, Rojgar Melas, and recruitment drives to connect trained youth with employment and National Apprenticeship (NAPS) opportunities.
